# Senior Applied AI Engineer

We are a small applied AI team building production LLM systems for clients: retrieval-augmented assistants, agents and voice workflows. You will design, build and ship these systems end to end.

## What you'll do
- Build RAG pipelines: ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, reranking and prompt design.
- Ship LLM features to production users and own their evaluation and monitoring.
- Build agentic workflows and integrate tools (including MCP servers).
- Deploy and operate services on AWS.

## What we're looking for
- 7+ years of professional Python experience (FastAPI, Django or similar).
- At least one LLM-based system shipped to production users.
- Hands-on AWS deployment experience (Docker, ECS/EKS, Terraform a plus).
- Evidence of current AI practice: evals, agents, recent models and frameworks.
- Comfortable owning a system from design through deployment.
